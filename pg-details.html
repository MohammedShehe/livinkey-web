Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2022

1. Information we collect and store

Livinkey collects and stores the following categories of personal data solely to operate PG accommodation services:

  • Tenant & Guest data: Full name, email, phone number, nationality, gender, residency status (national/international), room assignment, PG assignment, rent amount, security fee, payment dates, arrival date.
  • Identity & immigration documents: Passport, visa, arrival stamp, C-Form, e-FRRO, Aadhaar (tenant and parent), university ID, passport-size photo.
  • Payment & billing history: Rent, electricity, maintenance, other charges, fines, payment proofs, cash payment records, transaction IDs.
  • Maintenance requests: Issue type, description, service date, attached images.
  • Feedback & ratings: Tenant feedback on living experience, maintenance, communication, amenities, technology.
  • Admin data: Name, email, phone, role (super_admin / admin), ID document, permissions, login activity (OTP, reset tokens).

2. How your data is used

All collected data is used strictly for:

  • Managing tenant/guest profiles, room assignments, and billing.
  • Generating bills and processing payments (online, cash, partial).
  • Handling maintenance requests and support tickets.
  • Collecting feedback to improve PG services.
  • Authenticating admins via email + OTP and role-based permissions.
  • Sending service-related emails (bill notifications, OTPs, password resets, e-FRRO expiry alerts).

Data is never shared with third parties for marketing or unrelated purposes.

3. Document storage & retention

Uploaded identity and travel documents are stored securely in Cloudinary and are visible only to admins with granted access. Authorized admins can view, download, or delete documents directly from a tenant's profile. Documents are retained as long as the tenant's account is active or as required by applicable law.

4. Messaging data

Conversations between admins and tenants/guests are automatically removed 30 days after being sent. Admins may also delete messages earlier manually. Custom bill messages and QR codes expire after the configured validity period.

5. Cookies & tracking

The Livinkey admin console uses session tokens (JWT) for authentication. No persistent cookies are used for tracking or analytics.

6. Data security

Access to tenant data is protected through:

  • Role-based permissions (view, add, edit, delete per module).
  • Two-factor verification (email OTP) for every admin sign-in.
  • Secure hashing of passwords and OTPs (bcrypt).
  • Encrypted transmission via HTTPS.

7. Your rights

Tenants and guests may request:

  • Access to their personal data stored in the system.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion of their data (subject to legal and operational retention requirements).

To exercise these rights, contact your property admin or email livinkey@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the Livinkey platform after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.

Terms & Conditions

Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2022

1. Acceptance of terms

By using the Livinkey admin console, mobile apps (Tenant, Guest), and associated services, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please refrain from using the platform.

2. Description of service

Livinkey provides a comprehensive PG management platform that includes:

  • Admin Console: Manage tenants, guests, PGs, rooms, bills, payments, maintenance, documents, feedback, and notifications.
  • Tenant App: View rent status, pay bills, submit maintenance requests, upload documents, give feedback, view notifications.
  • Guest App: Browse PGs, view ratings, search, and contact property admins.

3. Admin account responsibility

Each admin is responsible for:

  • Keeping login credentials (email and password) confidential.
  • All actions performed under their account, including changes to tenant, guest, billing, document, and messaging records.
  • Immediately notifying Livinkey of any unauthorized use or security breach.

4. Permitted use & permissions

Access to each management module (tenants, guests, bills, PGs, maintenance, documents, feedback) is granted individually by a Super Admin. Permissions include:

  • View: Read-only access to records.
  • Add: Create new records.
  • Edit: Modify existing records.
  • Delete: Remove records (subject to audit and retention policies).

Permissions may be changed or revoked at any time without prior notice.

5. Data handling & confidentiality

Admins may only view or process tenant/guest data (identity documents, payment records, contact details, etc.) as required to operate the property. You must not:

  • Export or share personal data outside approved channels.
  • Use data for any purpose other than providing Livinkey services.
  • Store or process data insecurely.

6. Tenant & guest obligations

Tenants and guests agree to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information during registration.
  • Upload valid and authentic documents (passport, visa, Aadhaar, etc.).
  • Make timely payments as per bills generated.
  • Submit maintenance requests with accurate descriptions.
  • Provide honest feedback to help improve services.

Failure to comply may result in suspension or termination of access.

7. Billing & payments

Bills are generated monthly and include rent, electricity, maintenance, and other charges. Payment methods include:

  • Online: UPI QR code, payment links, partial payments.
  • Cash: Verified by admin with OTP confirmation.

Late fees (₹100/day) apply after the 7-day grace period. Fines are automatically calculated and notified via email.

8. Document submission

Tenants must upload required documents based on their residency:

  • International: Passport, visa, arrival stamp, C-Form, e-FRRO, passport-size photo, university ID.
  • National: Passport-size photo, tenant Aadhaar, parent Aadhaar, university ID.

Documents are verified by admins. Failure to upload valid documents may affect accommodation.

9. e-FRRO monitoring

For international tenants, e-FRRO expiry dates are tracked. Automated email notifications are sent at 7, 14, and 30 days before expiry. Tenants must renew their e-FRRO on time to avoid legal issues.

10. Maintenance requests

Tenants can submit maintenance requests (Plumber, Electrician, Carpenter, RO, AC, WiFi, Cleaning, C-Form, Check-out, Others). Requests are processed by admins and updated through pending → in-progress → completed statuses.

11. Feedback & ratings

Tenants may submit feedback once per stay, rating (1–10) categories such as living experience, maintenance, communication, amenities, and technology. Feedback is used to improve PG services and is visible to admins.
